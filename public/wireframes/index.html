Idea and Wireframes

TickrLab

A market dashboard that computes its own signals in the browser, publishes its own data from CI, and shows you how often it has been wrong.

React 19 · TypeScript GitHub Actions as backend lightweight-charts Zustand Cloudflare Workers AI Zero servers

Blocking, not dismissible by clicking outside, and recorded in localStorage so it appears once. The name does the positioning work: this is a lab, not a service.

1

Called a Technical Momentum Score, never "BUY" and never "CALL/PUT". Naming it after what it measures rather than what to do keeps it a measurement.

2

Raw inputs printed alongside the score. Anyone can recompute the number by hand from this panel — which is the definition of auditable, and the reason the LLM is never allowed to produce these figures.

3

The panel that makes the project. Publishing a 51% post-cost hit rate next to your own signal is the single most differentiating element here. It reframes the work from "I built a trading tool" to "I built a measurement instrument and measured it."

05 Copilot — narrator, not oracle

Scores are computed client-side and passed to the model as context. The model explains numbers it did not produce, so it cannot hallucinate a price, an RSI, or a hit rate.

1
Ask TickrLab
Why is NVDA showing bullish?
Its momentum score is +72, driven mostly by RSI. At 31.2 RSI(14) is near the oversold threshold of 30, contributing +31. EMA(20) sits above EMA(50) by 2.11 ATR (+22), MACD histogram has risen three sessions (+13), and volume is 1.81× its 20-day average (+6).
Worth knowing: this configuration has been directionally right 53.1% of the time over 412 historical signals — 51.4% after costs.
⚠ Not financial advice · appended by the UI, not the model
Should I buy it?
I can't answer that — TickrLab doesn't make buy or sell recommendations, and these scores have no demonstrated predictive value. I can explain what any indicator on screen measures, or how it's calculated.

Two scoped jobs: explain what's on screen, and teach mechanics. Should-I-buy, price predictions, and broker recommendations are refused.

2

A local regex classifier runs before any inference. It is the safety layer and the cost control — refusals cost nothing. Prompt-injection cases belong in the test suite, and saying so in the README is a strong current signal.

The disclaimer is appended by the UI, not requested in the prompt, so no amount of jailbreaking removes it.

What is RSI?
A 0–100 measure of how much of recent movement has been upward. Below 30 is conventionally "oversold", above 70 "overbought" — descriptions of recent behaviour, not forecasts.
